Where Branding & Furry Inspiration Meet

Hello! I'm Aya Adel, a Senior Graphic Designer & Visual Identity Specialist based in Ismailia, Egypt, working with visionary brands globally. With over 5 years in the creative industry, I help businesses discover their unique visual voice and translate it into unforgettable design touchpoints.

My work spans complete brand identity suites, luxury packaging die-lines, high-fashion editorial layouts, and high-converting social media creative kits. I obsess over fine details: the curve of a custom ligature, the tactile finish of textured paper, and the subtle emotional resonance of color palettes.

My creative companions: Outside the design studio, you will find me drawing inspiration from nature, fine art, and cuddling my three feline co-designers (Milo, Oliver, and Luna) who never fail to keep the studio lively and warm!
